00Trigger Dictionary

A trigger is a small grouping of moves your fingers learn as one unit. Chunk the alg in your head instead of memorizing letter soup.

01White Cross

Goal: Solve four white edges on the bottom (yes, bottom — you'll thank me later) so each edge matches its center.

Strategy for Speed

  1. Solve on the bottom, not the top. Flipping the cube wastes time, and you can't see your F2L corners while solving the cross on top.
  2. Plan in inspection. During the 15-second inspection, trace all four edges. Goal: zero pauses while solving.
  3. 8 moves or fewer. Every cross is solvable in 8 moves max (most in 6–7). If you're using 12+, you're not planning.
  4. Don't solve edge-by-edge. Look for pairs of edges that can be solved together with shared setup moves.
  5. Color neutrality (advanced). Eventually, solve the cross on whichever color is easiest — not just white. Halves your average inspection difficulty.

Drill

Practice cross-only solves. Time just the cross. Aim: sub-3 seconds consistently before stressing F2L.

02F2L — Intuitive

Goal: Solve a corner-edge pair into each of the four bottom slots, simultaneously.

The Big Idea

You're not memorizing 41 cases. You're learning one concept: pair up the corner and edge above the slot, then insert them together.

The Pairing Rule

Every F2L solve is just two questions:

  1. Where's the corner? (Top layer or bottom slot)
  2. Where's the edge? (Top layer or middle slot)

If both are stuck in wrong spots, pop them out to the top first, then pair.

The Three Pair-Up Templates

Template A — "Easy Pair"
Corner and edge both on top, ready to join.

If the corner is above its slot with white facing the side, and the edge is on top:

  • Move the edge next to the corner so they're aligned.
  • Insert with Sexy (R U R' U') or its mirror (L' U' L U).
Template B — "Hide and Seek"
White on top of the corner.
  • Set up so the pair forms after a U move.
  • Insertion uses an Insert trigger (R U R') after a setup U.
Template C — "Split Pair"
Pair is together but going in wrong.

The corner and edge are joined but the edge is on the wrong side. Use the Sledgehammer (R' F R F') to flip the pair, then insert.

Watch For

  • Already-paired in slot but flipped: Pull the pair out with an Insert, reset, re-insert.
  • Pair forms naturally: Sometimes a U move alone makes a perfect pair appear. Look before you move.
  • Empty slot, full top: Always solve the easiest pair first.

Drill

  • Slow F2L: Solve every F2L pair at 1 move per second, no pauses, no rotations. Speed comes from no pauses, not fast turning.
  • Cross + F2L only. Time these together. Goal: under 20s combined for a beginner.

032-Look OLL

Goal: Make the entire top face yellow. Two steps: orient edges (yellow cross), then orient corners (full yellow face).

Step 1: Edges (3 cases)

Dot Shape
No yellow edges face up.
F (R U R' U') F' f (R U R' U') f'
F — Sexy — F' — wide f — Sexy — wide f'
Two stacked F-wraps around Sexy, second one wide.
I-Shape (line)
A horizontal yellow line.
F (R U R' U') F'
F — Sexy — F'
One F-wrap around a Sexy.
L-Shape
Two yellow edges meeting at a corner.
f (R U R' U') f'
wide f — Sexy — wide f'
One wide-f-wrap around a Sexy. Same as I-Shape but wide.
Memory hook: I-Shape and L-Shape are the same Sexy in different wrappers. Dot is just both of them stacked.

Step 2: Corners (7 cases)

Sune
One yellow corner front-left, headlights on the back.
(R U R' U) (R U2 R')
Lifted Sexy — Double Insert
Antisune
One yellow corner front-right, headlights on the left.
(R U2 R' U') (R U' R')
Reverse Double Insert + U' — Reverse Insert
Reverse Double Insert, then U', then a Reverse Insert.
H
Two pairs of headlights opposite each other.
(R U R' U) (R U' R' U) (R U2 R')
Lifted Sexy — Backsexy — Double Insert
Sune's pattern with a Backsexy stuffed in the middle.
Pi (π)
No headlights. Symmetric — front half mirrors back half.
R U2 (R2 U' R2 U' R2) U2 R
R U2 — Triple-R2 Shuffle — U2 R
The middle R2 U' R2 U' R2 is its own thing — three R2s separated by U' moves.
T
Two yellow corners on the same side, headlights opposite.
(r U R' U') (r' F R F')
Fat Sexy — Fat Sledgehammer
Fat Sledgehammer = Sledgehammer that starts with a wide r' instead of R'.
L
Two yellow corners diagonally across with an L-shape of yellow on a side.
(F R' F' r) (U R U' r')
Fat Hedgeslammer — Reverse Insert (fat finish)
Fat Hedgeslammer = Hedgeslammer with a wide r at the end.
U
Two yellow corners on the back, U-shape of yellow on a side. The only OLL here that uses D.
R2 D (R' U2 R) D' (R' U2 R')
R2 — D-wrap around Reverse Double Insert — R' U2 R'
D wraps around a clean Reverse Double Insert. Closing R' U2 R' is almost a Reverse Double Insert but ends with R' instead of R.

042-Look PLL

Goal: Move pieces to their correct spots. Two steps: corners first, then edges.

Step 1: Corners (2 cases)

Find the headlights — two corners of the same color side-by-side.

Headlights (A-Perm style)
Hold headlights on the back. Three other corners cycle.
(R U R' U') R' F (R2 U' R' U') (R U R') F'
Sexy — R' F — R2 + Lefty Sexy chunk — Insert — F'
Diagonal
No headlights anywhere — all four corners need to move. Two diagonal corners swap.
F (R U' R' U') (R U R') F' (R U R' U') (R' F R F')
F — Lefty-Sexy chunk — Insert — F' — Sexy — Sledgehammer
Mnemonic: ends with Sexy + Sledge — easiest part to remember.

Step 2: Edges (4 cases)

Ua Perm
Three edges cycle counterclockwise. Hold solved edge in back.
R U' (R U R U) (R U' R' U') R2
R U' — U-Wave — Lefty Sexy — R2
U-Wave = R U R U, four moves alternating.
Ub Perm
Three edges cycle clockwise. Mirror of Ua. Hold solved edge in back.
R2 U (R U R' U') (R' U' R' U) R'
R2 U — Sexy — Lefty Lift — R'
Lefty Lift = R' U' R' U, a Lefty-Sexy mirror starting from R'.
H Perm
Opposite edges swap with each other.
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
No triggers — pure M-slices and U-moves. Chant it: M2-U-M2-U2-M2-U-M2.
Z Perm
Two pairs of adjacent edges swap.
M' U (M2 U M2 U) M' U2 M2
M' U — M-Wave — M' U2 M2
M-Wave = M2 U M2 U. Starts with M' instead of M2 — that's how you know it's Z, not H.

05Practice Roadmap

  1. Cross under 5s consistently → move on.
  2. F2L without rotations → speed comes later, smoothness first.
  3. Recognize OLL case in under 1 second → drill flashcards.
  4. PLL recognition by AUF (top-layer alignment) → never re-execute because of bad alignment.
  5. Sub-30 average is the first big milestone. Sub-20 means full PLL/OLL learning soon.
Cube on, and remember: smooth is fast.
